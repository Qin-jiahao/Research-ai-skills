# Paper Review

> Status: draft. Needs testing with complete manuscripts and section-level excerpts.

## Purpose

检查论文的研究问题、方法、证据链、逻辑结构和表达问题。它不替代同行评审。

## Workflow / 工作流程

1. 提取研究问题、假设、方法、结果和结论。
2. 检查主张是否有对应证据，区分事实、推断和过度解释。
3. 检查方法是否能够支持研究问题和统计结论。
4. 按严重程度输出问题、依据和可执行修改建议。

## Output / 输出

输出包括问题清单、严重程度、对应原文位置、修改建议和需要作者补充的信息。

## English

This Skill reviews research questions, methods, evidence alignment, logical structure, and academic expression. It does not replace peer review.

### Workflow and output

Extract the research question, hypotheses, methods, results, and conclusions; assess claim-evidence alignment and overinterpretation; examine whether the methods support the claims; and report issues by severity with locations, evidence, revision suggestions, and missing information.
