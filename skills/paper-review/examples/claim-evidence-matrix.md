# Claim–Evidence Matrix Example / 主张与证据矩阵示例

## Input / 输入

**Claim / 主张:** The intervention reduces symptom severity.

**Evidence / 证据:** The intervention group had a lower mean score at week 4. The study was observational.

## Expected output / 预期输出

将该问题列为 Major 或 Moderate，取决于研究设计和全文信息。指出观察性设计不能仅凭组间差异支持“reduces”的因果表述，建议改为描述性表述，并要求报告混杂因素处理方法。

Classify the issue as Major or Moderate depending on the full design. Explain that an observational group difference alone does not support the causal verb “reduces”. Recommend descriptive wording and request information about confounding control.
