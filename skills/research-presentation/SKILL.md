# Research Presentation / 科研汇报

**Maturity / 成熟度:** draft

## Purpose / 用途

帮助研究者把研究问题、证据和限制组织成可在规定时间内讲清楚的幻灯片结构。这个 Skill 适用于组会、开题、中期汇报、学位答辩、学术会议报告和项目汇报。它可以生成故事线、逐页大纲、图表使用建议、讲稿提示和核查清单。

This Skill helps researchers turn a research question, evidence, and limitations into a slide structure that can be explained within a fixed time. It applies to lab meetings, proposals, progress reports, thesis defenses, conference talks, and project presentations. It can produce a storyline, slide-by-slide outline, figure guidance, speaker cues, and review checklists.

它只使用用户提供的研究材料和可核验来源。它不能补写数据、样本量、统计结果、参考文献或研究结论，也不能把版式改进当作证据质量的证明。

It uses only the supplied research materials and verifiable sources. It must not fill in data, sample sizes, statistical results, references, or conclusions, and it must not treat visual polish as evidence of scientific quality.

## When to use / 适用场景

- 从论文、实验记录或项目材料制定汇报故事线和页序；
- 为开题、答辩、组会或会议报告分配时间和页面功能；
- 检查每个重要主张是否有相应的图表、数据或文献支持；
- 改善科研图表在投影、线上会议或打印场景下的可读性；
- 根据已有幻灯片准备讲者备注、问答准备和演练清单。

Use it to:

- build a storyline and slide order from a paper, lab record, or project brief;
- allocate time and slide roles for a proposal, defense, lab meeting, or conference talk;
- check whether each important claim has corresponding data, figures, tables, or literature;
- improve figure readability for projection, online meetings, or print; and
- prepare speaker notes, question-and-answer preparation, and rehearsal checks from an existing deck.

## Scope and boundaries / 范围与边界

这个 Skill 关注信息组织、证据呈现和可检查的沟通设计。它不判断实验是否真实有效，不替代统计分析、伦理审查、同行评审或专业设计审查。没有看到原始数据、完整图表或引用全文时，输出应限于结构建议和待核验事项。

This Skill focuses on information organization, evidence presentation, and checkable communication design. It does not validate experiments or replace statistical analysis, ethics review, peer review, or specialist design review. When raw data, complete figures, or full citation texts are unavailable, limit the output to structural advice and items to verify.

不要把“观众可能接受”写成研究结论，也不要把“统计显著”自动写成“具有实际意义”。因果措辞必须与研究设计和证据范围一致。

Do not present audience acceptance as a research finding, and do not equate statistical significance with practical importance. Causal wording must match the study design and the available evidence.

## Required inputs / 所需输入

尽量提供以下信息。缺失内容标记为“待补充”，不能用默认值或虚构事实填充。

Provide the following information where possible. Mark missing information as “to be provided” instead of filling it with defaults or invented facts.

1. **场景与受众 / Setting and audience**：汇报类型、听众的专业背景、预期互动方式和语言。
2. **时间约束 / Time constraint**：总时长、问答时长、是否需要备用页，以及是否有页数或模板限制。
3. **核心目标 / Presentation objective**：希望听众理解、判断或记住的具体内容。
4. **研究问题与设计 / Research question and design**：研究问题、假设或估计目标、研究对象、对照、主要变量和研究设计。
5. **证据材料 / Evidence**：已经确认的结果、样本量、效应量、不确定性、图表、表格、质量控制信息和分析方法。
6. **来源与引用 / Sources and citations**：文献、数据集、图片、软件或方法来源，及其许可和引用要求。
7. **表达约束 / Format constraints**：幻灯片比例、模板、字体、颜色、可访问性要求和是否需要可编辑源文件。
8. **已知风险 / Known risks**：结果不完整、替代解释、统计假设、潜在混淆因素和不能公开的内容。

如果用户只提供一张图或几句结论，先列出无法判断的事项，不要直接扩写成完整研究故事。

If the user supplies only one figure or a few conclusions, first list what cannot be determined. Do not expand it into a complete research story without evidence.

## Core workflow / 核心流程

### 1. Define the communication contract / 明确沟通契约

写出听众、场景、总时长、汇报目标和验收标准。验收标准应可检查，例如“听众能说出研究问题和主要证据”，而不是“让报告更有冲击力”。

State the audience, setting, total time, presentation objective, and acceptance criteria. Criteria should be checkable, such as “the audience can state the research question and main evidence,” rather than “make the talk more impactful.”

### 2. Reduce the study to a question and answer / 压缩为问题与回答

把研究材料整理为一条主线：背景或缺口、研究问题、方法、关键证据、解释范围、限制和下一步。区分“研究要回答什么”和“数据目前能支持什么”。如果主问题有多个，说明它们之间的层级，避免让每页承担互不相关的任务。

Reduce the material to one line: background or gap, research question, methods, key evidence, interpretation, limitations, and next steps. Separate what the study asks from what the current data support. If there are multiple questions, show their hierarchy instead of assigning unrelated tasks to every slide.

### 3. Build an evidence chain / 建立证据链

为每个重要主张记录对应的证据、分析方法、引用或限制。推荐使用以下字段：

For each important claim, record the corresponding evidence, analysis method, citation, and limitation. The following fields are useful:

```text
Claim / 主张:
Evidence / 证据:
Analysis or source / 分析或来源:
Strength and uncertainty / 证据强度与不确定性:
Permitted wording / 可使用的表述:
Open question / 待确认问题:
```

如果没有直接证据，使用“研究目的”“待检验假设”或“可能解释”等准确标签，不能使用确定性结论标题。

When direct evidence is unavailable, label the statement as a study objective, testable hypothesis, or possible interpretation. Do not use a definitive conclusion as the slide title.

### 4. Assign a role to every slide / 为每页确定功能

每页只承担一个主要沟通任务。可以使用问题、方法、结果、解释、限制、结论或备用页等功能标签。页标题应让听众知道这一页要回答什么；只有在证据足够时，标题才可以使用结论句。

Assign one primary communication role to each slide. Useful roles include question, method, result, interpretation, limitation, conclusion, and backup. The title should tell the audience what the slide answers. Use a conclusion sentence as the title only when the evidence supports it.

### 5. Allocate time and cognitive load / 分配时间和认知负荷

先按内容难度和证据重要性分配时间，再决定页数。关键结果需要留出解释坐标轴、样本量、不确定性和限制的时间。不要用连续动画、过密文字或多个无关图表压缩讲解时间。把详细方法、补充分析和完整参考文献放到备用页或附录，但仍保持可追溯。

Allocate time according to content difficulty and evidential importance before deciding the slide count. Reserve time to explain axes, sample size, uncertainty, and limitations for key results. Do not compress explanation with continuous animation, dense text, or unrelated figures. Put detailed methods, supplementary analyses, and full references in backup slides or an appendix while keeping them traceable.

### 6. Specify figures and tables / 规范图表

图表应服务于当前页的主张。检查坐标轴、单位、分组、样本量、误差或不确定性定义、统计标记、颜色含义和数据范围。保持源数据不变，优先使用可编辑图形。避免截断坐标轴、颜色对比不足、图例与图形分离、过度平滑和无法解释的装饰。图表无法在目标观看距离下辨认时，应减少元素或拆页。

Figures and tables should serve the claim on the current slide. Check axes, units, groups, sample size, error or uncertainty definitions, statistical annotations, color meaning, and data range. Keep source data unchanged and prefer editable graphics. Avoid truncated axes, weak color contrast, detached legends, unexplained smoothing, and decorative elements that imply evidence. If a figure cannot be read at the intended viewing distance, reduce elements or split the slide.

### 7. Add citations and provenance / 补充引用与来源

在使用外部结论、方法、图片、数据集、软件或改编图形的页面注明来源。页面脚注应足以定位来源，末页再给出完整参考文献。无法确认许可或原始出处的材料标记为待核查，不要把网上截图当作正式来源。

Credit external claims, methods, images, datasets, software, and adapted graphics on the slide where they are used. Use a locator-level citation in the footer and provide full references at the end. Mark uncertain permissions or provenance as items to verify; do not treat an online screenshot as a formal source.

### 8. Prepare notes and verify the deck / 编写讲者备注并核查

为每页写一句主旨、一到两个证据解释点、转场句和可能的问题。演练时核对总时长、数字和单位、图表与讲稿的一致性、引用是否完整，以及结论是否超出设计。将“已核查”“待核查”和“不能判断”分开记录。

For each slide, write one key message, one or two evidence points, a transition, and a likely question. During rehearsal, check total time, numbers and units, consistency between figures and notes, citation completeness, and whether the conclusion exceeds the design. Keep “checked,” “to be checked,” and “cannot determine” separate.

根据任务范围输出相关部分。缺失信息必须保留在“待确认问题”中。

## Quality checks / 质量检查

- 汇报目标、核心问题和核心结论是否一致；
- 每个关键主张是否有可追溯的证据或引用；
- 研究设计是否支持使用的因果、比较和推广措辞；
- 样本量、单位、误差或不确定性、统计方法和分母是否清楚；
- 图表能否在目标观看距离下读懂，颜色和标记是否有明确含义；
- 每页是否只有一个主要沟通任务，标题是否准确；
- 页数和讲解时间是否相符，是否保留解释限制的时间；
- 外部材料、改编图形、数据集和软件是否有来源及许可记录；
- 讲者备注、图表、数值和最终结论是否一致；
- 是否明确区分已核查内容、待核查内容和无法判断的内容。

- Are the presentation objective, research question, and take-home message aligned?
- Does every important claim have traceable evidence or a citation?
- Does the study design support the causal, comparative, and generalization language used?
- Are sample size, units, error or uncertainty, statistical method, and denominators clear?
- Can figures be read at the intended viewing distance, with meaningful colors and markers?
- Does each slide have one primary communication task and an accurate title?
- Do slide count and speaking time fit, with time reserved for limitations?
- Are sources and permissions recorded for external materials, adapted figures, datasets, and software?
- Are speaker notes, figures, numbers, and conclusions consistent?
- Are checked items, items to verify, and undetermined items clearly separated?

## Complete-input example / 完整输入示例

输入包含受众、总时长、研究问题、研究设计、主要结果、图表、引用来源和问答目标。输出应形成逐页大纲、主张—证据表、时间分配和演练核查清单。

The input contains the audience, total time, research question, study design, main results, figures, citations, and Q&A goal. The output should include a slide-by-slide outline, claim–evidence table, time allocation, and rehearsal checklist.

## Incomplete-input example / 信息不足示例

输入只有一张图和几句结论。输出应列出无法判断的样本量、单位、误差、统计方法、来源和受众信息，只给出条件化的版式建议，不扩写完整研究故事。

If the input contains only one figure and a few conclusions, list the audience, sample size, units, uncertainty, statistical method, and provenance as undetermined. Give conditional layout advice rather than expanding a complete research story.

## Limitations / 限制

这个 Skill 不能验证原始数据、统计模型或引用内容的真实性，也不能保证听众理解或报告通过答辩。它只能根据提供的材料提出结构和核查建议。最终的科学判断、数据解释和公开许可由研究者负责。

This Skill cannot validate the truth of raw data, statistical models, or citations, and it cannot guarantee audience understanding or defense acceptance. It can only provide structural and verification advice based on the supplied materials. Researchers remain responsible for scientific judgment, data interpretation, and permissions for public use.
