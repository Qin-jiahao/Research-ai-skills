# Literature Analysis

> Status: draft. Search results must be independently verified.

## Purpose

支持文献检索、筛选、证据整理和研究空白分析。它不能在没有检索结果或全文的情况下声称某篇文献存在。

## Required inputs / 所需输入

研究问题、关键词、时间范围、数据库、纳入与排除标准，以及需要提取的证据字段。

## Workflow / 工作流程

1. 将研究问题拆成检索概念和同义词。
2. 设计检索式并记录数据库、日期和筛选标准。
3. 按预设标准筛选文献，整理研究设计、样本、方法和主要发现。
4. 区分原文结论、研究者推断和证据不足之处。

## English

This Skill supports literature searching, screening, evidence extraction, and research-gap analysis. It must not claim that a paper exists without a verifiable search result or full text.

### Required inputs and workflow

Provide the research question, keywords, date range, databases, inclusion and exclusion criteria, and evidence fields. Decompose the question into concepts and synonyms, document the search strategy and date, screen studies against predefined criteria, extract study design and findings, and distinguish reported conclusions from inference.
