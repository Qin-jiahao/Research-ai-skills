# Research Plan and Handoff / 科研计划与交接

## 中文

请根据我提供的研究问题、设计、数据、资源和时间限制，先检查信息是否足够，再输出可核验的研究计划。请区分已提供事实、工作假设、待核验事项和未执行步骤。计划至少包含研究问题、证据边界、风险、里程碑、验收标准、当前状态、阻塞点、下一步和禁止重复的失败路径。不要编造样本量、结果、文献、时间或完成状态。信息不足时暂停相关步骤并列出需要补充的字段。

## English

Based only on the research question, design, data, resources, and time limits I provide, first check completeness and then produce a verifiable research plan. Separate supplied facts, working assumptions, unresolved items, and unexecuted steps. Include the question, evidence boundary, risks, milestones, acceptance criteria, current state, blockers, next steps, and failed paths that should not be repeated. Do not invent sample sizes, results, references, dates, or completion status. If information is missing, pause the affected step and list the fields required.
