# Research Plan and Handoff / 科研计划与交接

## 中文

请根据我提供的研究问题、设计、数据、资源和时间限制，先检查信息是否足够，再输出可核验的研究计划。请区分已提供事实、工作假设、待核验事项和未执行步骤。计划至少包含研究问题、证据边界、风险、里程碑、验收标准、当前状态、阻塞点、下一步和禁止重复的失败路径。不要编造样本量、结果、文献、时间或完成状态。信息不足时暂停相关步骤并列出需要补充的字段。

## English

Based only on the research question, design, data, resources, and time limits I provide, first check completeness and then produce a verifiable research plan. Separate supplied facts, working assumptions, unresolved items, and unexecuted steps. Include the question, evidence boundary, risks, milestones, acceptance criteria, current state, blockers, next steps, and failed paths that should not be repeated. Do not invent sample sizes, results, references, dates, or completion status. If information is missing, pause the affected step and list the fields required.

## Prompt contract / 提示词契约

### 中文

- **使用场景：** 将已有研究问题、设计和资源整理为可执行、可验收的计划和交接记录。
- **输入要求：** 研究问题、研究设计、数据与材料、资源、时间限制、交付物和当前状态。
- **输出要求：** 信息完整性表、证据边界、里程碑、风险、验收标准、阻塞点、下一步和失败路径。
- **禁止编造：** 不得编造样本量、结果、文献、日期、资源可用性或完成状态。
- **信息不足：** 先列出缺失字段并暂停受影响步骤，只输出条件化计划。
- **双语示例：** 完整输入含研究问题、数据清单和时间限制，输出里程碑与验证节点；信息不足时只有“比较两组”，输出待补充字段，不选择模型。

### English

- **Use case:** Turn a supplied question, design, and resources into an executable, checkable plan and handoff.
- **Required inputs:** Research question, design, data and materials, resources, time limits, deliverables, and current state.
- **Expected output:** Completeness table, evidence boundary, milestones, risks, acceptance criteria, blockers, next steps, and failed paths.
- **Fabrication ban:** Do not invent sample sizes, results, references, dates, resource availability, or completion status.
- **Missing information:** List missing fields and pause affected steps; return a conditional plan only.
- **Bilingual example:** With a question, data inventory, and time limit, return milestones and verification points; with only “compare two groups,” return missing fields and do not choose a model.
