# Figure Evidence Audit / 科研图表证据审查

## 中文

请根据我提供的数据、变量定义、研究问题、分析输出和格式要求，制定或审查图表方案。先列出缺失信息。对每张图说明主要主张、数据来源、统计单位、分组、坐标轴和单位、样本量、误差或不确定性、统计方法、图注、来源和可编辑输出格式。不要仅凭截图生成图，不要编造数据或显著性，不要把相关性写成因果关系。输出中区分已检查、待核验和无法判断。

## English

Using only the data, variable definitions, research question, analytical outputs, and format requirements I provide, create or audit a figure plan. List missing information first. For each figure, state the main claim, data source, statistical unit, groups, axes and units, sample size, uncertainty, statistical method, caption, provenance, and editable output format. Do not generate figures from screenshots alone, invent data or significance, or turn association into causation. Separate checked, to-be-verified, and undetermined items.

## Prompt contract / 提示词契约

### 中文

- **使用场景：** 绘图前审查图表主张、数据、方法和来源的对应关系。
- **输入要求：** 数据或已核验结果、变量定义、研究问题、分析输出、格式和许可证信息。
- **输出要求：** 每张图的主张—证据表、图形规格、缺失信息和核查清单。
- **禁止编造：** 不得从截图生成数据、显著性、样本量或因果关系。
- **信息不足：** 先列出无法判断的数据、单位、分组、统计方法和来源，只给条件化方案。
- **双语示例：** 完整输入包括数据字典和统计输出，输出图表规格与图注；信息不足时只有截图，输出待补充字段，不生成图或结论。

### English

- **Use case:** Audit the links among figure claims, data, methods, and provenance before plotting.
- **Required inputs:** Data or checked results, variable definitions, research question, analytical outputs, format, and permission information.
- **Expected output:** A claim–evidence table, figure specification, missing-information list, and audit checklist for each figure.
- **Fabrication ban:** Do not derive data, significance, sample size, or causality from a screenshot.
- **Missing information:** List undetermined data, units, groups, methods, and provenance first and return only a conditional plan.
- **Bilingual example:** With a data dictionary and statistical output, return a specification and caption; with only a screenshot, return missing fields and no figure or conclusion.
