# Figure Evidence Audit / 科研图表证据审查

## 中文

请根据我提供的数据、变量定义、研究问题、分析输出和格式要求，制定或审查图表方案。先列出缺失信息。对每张图说明主要主张、数据来源、统计单位、分组、坐标轴和单位、样本量、误差或不确定性、统计方法、图注、来源和可编辑输出格式。不要仅凭截图生成图，不要编造数据或显著性，不要把相关性写成因果关系。输出中区分已检查、待核验和无法判断。

## English

Using only the data, variable definitions, research question, analytical outputs, and format requirements I provide, create or audit a figure plan. List missing information first. For each figure, state the main claim, data source, statistical unit, groups, axes and units, sample size, uncertainty, statistical method, caption, provenance, and editable output format. Do not generate figures from screenshots alone, invent data or significance, or turn association into causation. Separate checked, to-be-verified, and undetermined items.
