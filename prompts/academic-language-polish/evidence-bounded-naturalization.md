# Evidence-bounded Naturalization / 有证据边界的自然化润色

## 中文

### 使用场景

在论文、研究报告或科研沟通文本出现机器翻译感、句子过长、连接词堆叠或表达不自然时使用。目标是改善可读性，不是规避检测，也不是替作者改变立场。

### 输入要求

请提供原文、文本用途、目标读者、必须保留的术语和数字、引用范围，以及可以接受的语气变化。最好提供一段作者自己的写作样本，但不能要求模仿特定作者。

### 输出要求

先列出事实、数字、引用和主张，再给出修改稿、关键修改说明和待作者确认项。修改稿应保留原始含义、证据强度、限定语和作者观点。

### 禁止编造的内容

不得新增数据、文献、因果关系、引文、作者经历或研究结论。不得删除限制条件来让文字显得更有说服力，也不得承诺文本一定能通过任何检测器。

### 信息不足时的处理方式

缺少上下文时只做句法和词语层面的保守修改，并标出可能改变含义的句子。无法判断术语含义时保留原词并请求定义。

### 双语示例

#### 中文输入

请把一段关于相关性分析的英文改得更自然，但我没有提供原始数据和统计方法。

#### 中文输出要点

可以改善语法和句式，但不能把“相关”改成“导致”，也不能补写效应量或 P 值。统计方法和数据限制应列为待确认项。

#### English input

Make a paragraph about a correlation analysis sound more natural. The raw data and statistical method are not provided.

#### English output points

Grammar and sentence structure may be improved, but “correlated with” must not become “caused,” and no effect size or P value may be added. The missing method and data limitations must be listed for confirmation.

## English

### Use case

Use this Prompt when a manuscript, research report, or scientific message sounds machine-translated, overly long, or mechanically connected. The goal is readability, not detector evasion or a change in the author's position.

### Required inputs

Provide the source text, purpose, audience, terms and numbers that must remain unchanged, citation scope, and acceptable tone changes. A sample of the author's own writing can help, but do not imitate a named author.

### Expected output

List facts, numbers, citations, and claims first. Then return a revision, key changes, and items for author confirmation. Preserve meaning, evidence strength, qualifiers, and the author's view.

### Fabrication ban

Do not add data, references, causal claims, quotations, personal experience, or conclusions. Do not remove limitations to make the prose more persuasive, and do not promise detector evasion.

### Missing information

With missing context, make conservative grammar-level edits and flag sentences whose meaning could change. Keep undefined technical terms and request their definitions.

### Bilingual example

The example above is bilingual and must preserve the correlation-versus-causation boundary in both languages.
