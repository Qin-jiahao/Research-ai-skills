# Open-source Reuse Review / 开源项目复用审查

## 中文

请根据我提供的科研任务、候选项目链接、运行环境和许可证要求，审查直接使用、二次开发和自行实现三种路径。请核对官方文档、版本、维护状态、许可证、依赖、数据访问和安全边界，并记录访问日期和证据链接。不要把截图、star 数、宣传语或评论当作质量证据。无法访问或无法核验的项目标记为“待核验”，不要给出确定性结论。

## English

Given the research task, candidate project links, execution environment, and license requirements I provide, compare direct use, adaptation, and independent implementation. Check official documentation, versions, maintenance, licenses, dependencies, data access, and security boundaries, recording access dates and evidence links. Do not treat screenshots, star counts, promotional language, or comments as quality evidence. Mark inaccessible or unverifiable projects as “to be verified” and avoid definitive conclusions.
