# Claim–Evidence Check / 主张与证据检查

## 中文

请检查下面论文片段中的“主张—证据—结论”关系。对每个主要主张建立一行记录，包含：原文位置、主张内容、对应证据、证据是否充分、是否存在过度解释，以及具体修改建议。

请把问题分为 Major、Moderate 和 Minor。不要判断数据是否真实，也不要补写缺失结果。若证据不足，请明确写出需要作者补充的材料。

## English

Review the claim–evidence–conclusion relationships in the manuscript excerpt below. For each major claim, record its location, claim, supporting evidence, whether the evidence is sufficient, any overinterpretation, and a specific revision suggestion.

Classify issues as Major, Moderate, or Minor. Do not judge data authenticity or invent missing results. When evidence is insufficient, state exactly what the author needs to provide.

## Prompt contract / 提示词契约

### 中文

- **使用场景：** 检查论文片段中的主张、证据和结论是否匹配。
- **输入要求：** 论文片段、位置标记、研究设计、结果表或图、引用和审查范围。
- **输出要求：** 按 Major、Moderate、Minor 分级的主张—证据表、影响和具体修改建议。
- **禁止编造：** 不得补写缺失结果、引用、样本量或数据真实性判断。
- **信息不足：** 明确列出需要作者提供的原始表格、方法、比较对象或引用全文。
- **双语示例：** 完整输入含段落和对应图表，输出逐条证据审查；信息不足时只有结论句，输出无法判断项，不替作者补证据。

### English

- **Use case:** Check whether claims, evidence, and conclusions in a manuscript excerpt align.
- **Required inputs:** Excerpt, location markers, study design, result tables or figures, citations, and review scope.
- **Expected output:** A Major/Moderate/Minor claim–evidence table with impact and concrete revisions.
- **Fabrication ban:** Do not supply missing results, references, sample sizes, or judgments about data authenticity.
- **Missing information:** State which raw tables, methods, comparators, or full citations the author must provide.
- **Bilingual example:** With a paragraph and its figure, return an itemized evidence audit; with only a conclusion sentence, list undetermined items and do not invent support.
