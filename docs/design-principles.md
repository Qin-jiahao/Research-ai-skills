# Design Principles

## 中文

每个 Skill 都应明确任务边界、输入要求、工作流程、输出格式和质量检查。遇到信息不足时，应先提出缺失信息，不应直接补全关键科研条件。

## English

Every Skill should define its scope, required inputs, workflow, output format, and quality checks. When essential information is missing, it should identify the gap rather than inventing research conditions.
