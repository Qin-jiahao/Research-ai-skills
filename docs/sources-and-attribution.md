# Sources and Attribution

## 中文

本项目中的内容分为三类：

1. **原创内容**：由项目维护者重新设计、编写和测试，适用仓库根目录的许可证。
2. **已知来源内容**：明确知道原作者或原仓库时，保留作者、项目链接和原始许可证，并在对应文件中标注。
3. **来源不明内容**：只作为整理和改写的参考，不复制原文，并统一标注：`来源于网络，自行整理汇总`。

仓库根目录的许可证只适用于项目维护者拥有版权的原创部分，不会覆盖第三方内容的原始权利。

## English

Materials in this project are classified into three groups:

1. **Original content**: redesigned, written, and tested by the maintainers, and covered by the repository license.
2. **Materials with known sources**: the original author, project link, and license are retained in the relevant file.
3. **Materials with unclear provenance**: used only as references for independent organization and rewriting, never copied verbatim, and marked as: `Source: online materials, independently organized and summarized`.

The repository license applies only to original content owned by the maintainers. It does not override the rights attached to third-party materials.
